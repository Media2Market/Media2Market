MEDIA TO MARKET · RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Where rhetoric becomes signal.

Media to Market studies whether the rhetorical form of statements by influential political, economic and corporate figures carries measurable information about subsequent market behaviour — independent of how important the underlying event already was.

THE HYPOTHESIS

Does language add information?

The core research question is not whether news moves markets — it is whether the rhetorical form of a statement adds explanatory or predictive information beyond the underlying event alone. A high-rhetoric statement that produces no market move is a valid, informative result, not a failure of the model.

METHODOLOGY

How Rhetoric Intensity is computed

Rhetoric Intensity is the plain average of whichever applicable features — certainty, escalation, urgency, hedging, novelty, confidence, threat/coercion, policy commitment — were scored for that statement. It is calculated deterministically, not asked of the model as a separate number.

  • Optimism/pessimism is tracked separately as a direction, not averaged into intensity
  • Threat/coercion and policy commitment are null (excluded) when not applicable, not scored as zero
  • Analysis Confidence measures the model's confidence in its own classification — a different thing from the speaker's expressed confidence
SCAN SOURCES
Media to Market is an experimental research and analytics project. It does not provide investment advice or trading signals. All scores are model-generated research features validated against real search results where possible, and should not be treated as predictive until tested against structured market data. "Research signal," "association," and "forward hypothesis" describe observed patterns, not forecasts.
