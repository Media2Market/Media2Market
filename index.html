THE HYPOTHESIS

Does language add information?

The core research question is not whether news moves markets. It is whether the rhetorical form of a statement — certainty, escalation, urgency, hedging, confidence and novelty — adds useful explanatory or predictive information beyond the underlying event itself.

VALIDATION ROADMAP

From prototype to evidence

Future versions should ingest primary-source transcripts, calculate market returns from structured data at fixed windows, benchmark abnormal returns, maintain a persistent database and test whether rhetoric features improve prediction compared with event-only baselines.

SCAN SOURCES
Media to Market is an experimental analytics and research project. It does not provide investment advice or trading signals. Scores are model-generated research features and should be validated against structured market data before any predictive claims are made.
